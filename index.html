    Privacy and safety

    • Your photos never leave your device. The card reading happens right here on your phone.
    • No account, no passwords, no ads, no tracking.
    • The only thing sent out is the card name, to free card databases, to look up the card.
    • Prices are a guide only. Real sale prices change with condition and grading.

    Card data: Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, Lorcast. Exchange rates: Frankfurter. Text reading: Tesseract.js. This app is not made or endorsed by any card company or by eBay.
